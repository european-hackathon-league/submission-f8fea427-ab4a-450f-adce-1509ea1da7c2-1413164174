# G68 actual-vehicle asset search

**Result: no exact G68 3D asset found that is both publicly downloadable and published under a clearly permissive license.** Search checked the exact long-wheelbase family and named powertrains; no model is recommended for the viewer. Search conducted 10 October 2026.

## Vehicle identity and exact scope

G68 is the China-market, long-wheelbase 5 Series/i5 sedan; it is a distinct code from regular-wheelbase G60. BMW’s PressClub calls the 2024 i5 eDrive35L “exclusively from China for China” and tags it G68 / Long Wheelbase ([BMW PressClub photo page](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/photo/detail/P90535977/The-new-BMW-i5-eDrive35L-%E2%80%93-exclusively-from-China-for-China-01-2024)). BMW China’s official spec sheet names eDrive35L, eDrive40L and xDrive50L variants, while the 2025 Japanese-market LWB spec lists 525Li and i5 eDrive35L at 5,175 mm overall length and 3,105 mm wheelbase ([BMW China spec sheet](https://www.bmw.com.cn/zh_CN/all-models/bmw-i/i5/2024/specsheet.html), [BMW Japan LWB spec PDF](https://www.bmw.co.jp/content/dam/bmw/marketJP/bmw_co_jp/pdf/all-models/5/5series_Sedan_LWB_EPL_202509V1.pdf)). Those variants must not be mistaken for G60 530e/i5 or the global-wheelbase G60 i5 M60.

## 3D catalog coverage checked

- **Sketchfab public API**, direct unauthenticated model-search endpoint, queried `BMW G68`, `BMW 530Li`, `BMW i5 eDrive35L`, `BMW 530Li G68`, `BMW 5 series long wheelbase`, `BMW 5 series`, and `BMW 5 Series 2024`. Exact G68, 530Li, eDrive35L, and long-wheelbase searches returned no relevant BMW model. Broad `BMW 5 series` returned 24 results; the current-generation-looking results were G30 (2021), a non-downloadable 2024 BMW 5-Series by VeesGuy, and a non-downloadable 2024 BMW G60 by SLGA. The API’s `BMW G68` results were unrelated hardware parts, not cars. Neither of the 2024 models was available to download or carried an open license in API metadata.
- Sketchfab API search URL pattern used: `https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/search?type=models&q=<query>` (public metadata; not scraping an asset viewer or download endpoint). Downloadable historical 5 Series results are older G30/E generations, not G68.
- General web searches included exact English/Chinese model terms (`"G68 530Li" 3D model`, `BMW G68 3D model`, `i5 eDrive35L 3d model`, `宝马 G68 530Li 3D模型 下载`) and Sketchfab/CAD/model-catalog terms. Results were old-generation, generic BMW, paid, or non-3D-print assets; no exact openly downloadable G68 model surfaced.
- A 2024 BMW i5 model at 3D-Baza is **G60, not G68**, is paid, and is 89.74 MB / 147,226 polygons with 3ds Max, FBX, and OBJ files. It is not free/open and is not the long-wheelbase vehicle ([listing and generation statement](https://3d-baza.com/bmw-i5-2024-car-393441)).
- A free 520d BMW 5 Series on CadNav is unspecified generation and expressly **Non-commercial**, so it does not meet this use case ([CadNav listing](https://www.cadnav.com/3d-models/model-35884.html)).

No G68 candidate had a verifiable file archive, format list, polycount, texture count, or cabin content: no G68 3D download was available to inspect. Preview-only pages, unlicensed reposts, paid models, and unrelated G60 assets are not substitutes.

## Usable authentic image fallback (not 3D)

Wikimedia Commons has two directly photographed **BMW 525Li (G68)** photos by JustAnotherCarDesigner, dated 13 July 2024 and released under **CC0 1.0**: [photo 001](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BMW_525Li_(G68)_001.jpg), original 3,848×2,886 JPEG (2.98 MB); [photo 002](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BMW_525Li_(G68)_002.jpg), original 4,032×3,024 JPEG (3.97 MB). These are genuine G68 reference photos, but cannot provide orbit, cabin geometry, or 3D feedback mapping. Their listed model is 525Li; do not label them as an i5 eDrive35L.

BMW PressClub also offers downloadable high-resolution official eDrive35L photos (for example [P90535977](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/photo/detail/P90535977/The-new-BMW-i5-eDrive35L-%E2%80%93-exclusively-from-China-for-China-01-2024), 3.25 MB). These are **not open-license assets**: BMW MediaPool says its media are BMW AG copyrighted and permitted only for agreed purposes; other companies must obtain written permission ([BMW MediaPool media rights](https://mediapool.bmwgroup.com/download/edown/common/info?actEvent=mediaRights)). So they are not a permissive-license alternative for this viewer.

## Recommendation

For accurate G68 visuals today, use one of the CC0 525Li photographs as a clearly static image reference (credit appreciated, not required by CC0), or use the BMW official press images only after BMW grants written use permission. Keep the actual 3D view on a separately labeled, verified G60 model until an exact G68 model with an explicit redistribution license and downloadable file can be inspected. Do not claim an unverified G68 interior or long-wheelbase 3D asset.
