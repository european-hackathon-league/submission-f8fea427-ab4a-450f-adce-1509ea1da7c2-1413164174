Research supports claims about current capabilities and future assumptions. Competitor promotions do not prove customer demand. Store the accepted source capture and rejected applicability reasons.

Minimum record contracts

Case: program, vehicle, market, observation period, successor horizon, powertrain and configuration limits. Evidence: immutable source version, locator, original excerpt/value, applicability, translation where used. Finding: interpretation, supporting/contradicting evidence, distinct-record counts, limitations. Requirement version: customer outcome, finding links, factors/policy, evidence assessment, future assumptions, uncertainties and validation approach.

Where the PM controls the product

Review invariants we must implement

  • Approve only the exact version that the PM has inspected. Detect concurrent edits and require renewed review.
  • A change to wording, material evidence, assumptions, ratings or policy creates a proposed version. Preserve previous approvals.
  • Write the decision and its audit event in one transaction. Retried commands do not duplicate decisions.
  • Record who or what initiated the action, timestamp, reason, relevant before/after references and source versions.
  • Keep evidence strength, priority and decision status separate. High priority with weak evidence remains a validation opportunity.
  • An unresolved challenge can remain unresolved. The system can defend a proposal when the evidence supports it.
