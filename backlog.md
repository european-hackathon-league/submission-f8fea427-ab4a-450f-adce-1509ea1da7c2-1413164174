# Deferred pipeline capabilities

The current product analyzes files uploaded for a run and supports PM edits and reruns against those inputs. The following capabilities are deferred, based on the revised product direction.

## New evidence and source refresh

- Scheduled imports, source connectors, source refresh and continuous monitoring.
- Detecting newly arrived evidence and creating ongoing review tasks.
- Comparing successive source snapshots and determining which existing proposals need reconsideration.

## Automatic reconsideration thresholds

- Rules for when new evidence should trigger reanalysis or a proposed requirement revision.
- Separate policies for software, physical product and other issue types, if justified by evaluation.
- Define the denominator, minimum evidence, deduplication and policy ownership before using affected-customer thresholds. Feedback records must not be represented as unique people without respondent identifiers.
- Revisit how these rules operate in autonomous and guided modes when ongoing evidence ingestion is implemented.

## Current scope boundary

- Initial uploads remain in scope, including multiple regional files and partial evidence packages.
- Deep Dive is an initial run option: research competitor approaches and public feedback for identified customer needs, capture the evidence, and use it in proposals. This bounded research is in scope; ongoing external-source refresh remains deferred.
- PM edits, exclusions, additions, cohort grouping and alternative proposals remain in scope.
- Rerun only affected analysis using the current source snapshot; preserve previous results and decisions.
- Autonomous completion is the primary path. Optional PM checkpoints are a secondary mode of the same pipeline.
- Automotive lifecycle scope remains market research/needs analysis and product planning (steps 1 and 2).
- A separate cross-model or cross-market comparison workspace is not planned. Regional differences within uploaded evidence remain relevant to conflict detection and proposal variants. Deep Dive competitor comparisons appear within the relevant findings and proposal evidence.
