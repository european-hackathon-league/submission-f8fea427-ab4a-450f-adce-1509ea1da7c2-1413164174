What changes, and why

Same direction as the workshop preview: a large vehicle, clear next action, supporting detail on demand. FWD naming follows that preview; existing production front page still uses FORM.

  • Remove journey navigation, chapter dots, progress counter and scroll prompts. Family selection already provides direct access to every model.
  • Replace 24 repeated image cards with four family choices and a variant selector. All current variants remain available.
  • Keep one Open workshop button. The chosen model gives it context; no repeated header or bottom call to action.
  • Keep one secondary 360° entry. Move the standalone iX reference into the workshop’s vehicle reference tools if still needed; it is not part of the 24-model dataset.
  • Remove slogans, persona labels and oversized model codes. Keep the vehicle itself as the visual focus.
  • Keep provenance and representative-image disclosure. Move engineering codes to workshop vehicle details.

Proposal replaces the long scroll journey with immediate selection. If the cinematic introduction remains important, keep it as an optional experience reached from the footer.
