# G60 i5 M60 cabin decision

Checked 10 October 2026 by parallel Luna high research agents and primary visual/source review. Goal: production G60 i5 M60 exterior and detailed interactive cabin. No purchase, seller contact, or paid mesh inspection occurred.

## Recommended candidate, conditional on license

[ARKVIZ BMW i5 M60 G60 2024](https://arkviz.net/3d/3d_cars/BMW/BMW_G60_2024_i5_M60/index.html), item MG60, **€90**. Creator lists 105,381 polygons and 129,290 vertices; Blender 4.0, FBX, OBJ, C4D, MAX and DAE; 4K texture channels. No GLB is listed. Native C4D lighting/animation is not guaranteed to survive web conversion.

Primary reviewer inspected creator's [cockpit render](https://arkviz.net/3d/3d_cars/BMW/BMW_G60_2024_i5_M60/10.jpg) and [rear-cabin render](https://arkviz.net/3d/3d_cars/BMW/BMW_G60_2024_i5_M60/12.jpg). Visible details include M steering wheel/buttons, curved displays, interaction bar, console/controller, front/rear seats, door cards and rear climate controls. These establish a strong visual lead, not verified topology or an independently inspected file. Polygon counts are seller claims, not measured triangle counts.

[ARKVIZ terms §5](https://arkviz.net/terms.html) grant personal use only and require prior written consent for modification, distribution or providing content to others. Commercial browser delivery and GLB conversion are **not cleared**. Recommendation: obtain explicit rights and a cabin wireframe/sample before purchase; then inspect and convert. Do not substitute the seller's render for an interactive model.

## BlendSwap and free search

Checked both pages of the supplied [BMW catalog](https://blendswap.com/3d/bmw) ([page 2](https://blendswap.com/3d/bmw/2)), reporting 42 tagged listings. No exact G60/i5 listing appeared. [M4 F82](https://blendswap.com/blend/16463) is CC BY with a basic cabin, but the wrong generation/body; its creator also acknowledges accuracy problems. Old M3, 1 Series M, i8 and Z4 assets cannot stand in for G60.

Sketchfab, GitHub, creator portfolios, BlenderKit/Blendkit, CGTrader, 3DExport, TurboSquid, Hum3D/3DModels.org, ArtStation and free mirrors produced no verified freely downloadable, appropriately licensed detailed G60 i5 M60 cabin. This is a search outcome, not proof none exists. Freecreat lacks clear reuse rights; concepts, game rips and wrong-generation cars were excluded.
