# Blender fallback brief: BMW i5 M60 (G60)

Build an original Blender model of the 2024 BMW i5 M60 sedan, G60 generation. Use licensed references from multiple angles. Match production proportions and M60 details: lighting, grille, bumpers, wheels, glazing, handles, and trim. Separate M60 cues from shared G60 bodywork. Do not claim factory CAD accuracy or use BMW CAD without rights.

Create real cabin geometry: steering wheel, dashboard and curved displays, interaction bar, front and rear seats, console, and door cards. Keep parts separate with semantic names (`Body_Panels`, `Glass`, `Wheels_FL`, `Dashboard`, `Steering_Wheel`, `Seats_Front`). Use clean UVs, PBR materials, and licensed textures.

Deliver `.blend` source and optimized `.glb`. Measure against targets: under 250k triangles, under 20 MB, textures no larger than 2K, sensible material count, correct scale/origin, and no missing textures. Do not sacrifice silhouette or cabin to hit limits; document exceptions.

Review six exterior views plus front- and rear-cabin views at presentation scale. Check doors, windows, wheels, materials, labels, and GLB loading in the target viewer. Keep heat overlays as UI annotations unless geometry mapping is verified. Label it a visual prototype, not factory CAD.
